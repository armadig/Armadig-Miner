Pearl, Quantus and every card in one window

One program, one window, all of your cards. Each card mines the coin that pays more on it.

Start with just your addresses

armadig-miner.exe YOUR_PEARL_WALLET YOUR_QTC_WALLET

The coin of each address is recognised from its format, in either order. One address mines that coin only. The same works in the config file (wallet = ADDR1, ADDR2) and as one line in a HiveOS or RaveOS flight sheet.

How a card picks its coin

With both coins configured, each card mines the coin that pays more on that card: the card's own measured rate, times the coin's network reward per hash, times the coin price, minus the card's electricity at its power point (electricity only if you entered a tariff, see Profit mode).

  • A card moves to the other coin only when that pays at least 3 % more, and at most once per 30 minutes. The work in progress finishes and its shares are submitted first.
  • If a coin's price is older than 6 hours, switching pauses.
  • If a card's pool sends no work for 5 minutes, that card moves to the other coin at once.
  • The dev fee stays 0.75 % of the work of each coin, also across moves.

Pin a coin to a card

Do not want a card to switch? Pin it. A pinned card never switches; the others keep the automatic choice. It needs that coin's wallet.

armadig-miner.exe mine --pearl-wallet YOUR_PEARL_WALLET --qtc-wallet YOUR_QTC_WALLET --coin-map 0=pearl,1=qtc
armadig-miner.exe mine --pearl-wallet ... --qtc-wallet ... --gpu-pool 1=POOL_HOST:PORT

In armadig.conf: gpu0 = pearl, gpu1 = qtc, POOL_HOST:PORT. --algo pearl or --algo qtc pins every card to one coin.

Which cards, which pools

armadig-miner.exe --list                       # what each card would run
armadig-miner.exe YOUR_PEARL_WALLET YOUR_QTC_WALLET --gpu 0,2     # only cards 0 and 2
armadig-miner.exe mine --pearl-pool POOL_HOST:PORT --pearl-wallet W1 --qtc-pool POOL_HOST:PORT --qtc-wallet W2

Without a pool the miner uses the fastest approved pool for each coin. Name a pool with --pool kryptex (or luckypool, herominers). Pools can also be set in the config file: pool = qtc my.pool.net:7049, pearl kryptex.

Market data and privacy

Coin prices (several public sources, the median; one source down does not matter), network reward and difficulty, and the exchange rate of your currency are fetched every 10 or 30 minutes. The requests carry no wallet, pool, worker, card or rig name and no key. --proxy applies to them. With --fixed no market request is made and nothing switches.
