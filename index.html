What it does

Pearl, Quantus, every card, one window

Pass your two wallets. One program runs all your cards and puts each card on the coin that pays more on it.

How it works → Profit mode

Enter your electricity tariff and currency. The miner shows cost and profit per card and picks the best power limit for each tariff zone.

How it works → Tuned per card

Every card is tuned once, while mining, and the result is cached for the next start.
